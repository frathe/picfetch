# Interactive-map OSM attribution research

Checked 2026-10-01 against live primary sources. Research only; implementation and tile-service policy are outside scope.

## Finding

**Plain on-map credit plus Help/manual links is not unconditionally confirmed sufficient.** A separate licence link is expressly permitted when the attribution method cannot link; whether deliberately choosing a non-clickable label qualifies is unspecified. [OSMF interactive-map guidance](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines#Interactive_maps)

## Clear requirements and permitted alternatives

- OSM requires both displayed credit and an indication that the data is available under ODbL. Linking to its copyright page can supply the latter; plain contributor credit alone does not communicate the licence. [OSM copyright page, “How to credit OpenStreetMap”](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
- `© OpenStreetMap contributors` is accepted wording. Safe-harbour attribution must be legible, presented to users, and provide access to origin/licence information. A clickable credit is an example, not an absolute requirement. [OSMF general requirements and attribution text](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines#Requirements_to_fit_within_OSMF%E2%80%99s_safe_harbour)
- Interactive-map credit normally appears in a corner; adjacent credit or startup attribution is also allowed. When the attribution method cannot link, the licence link must be easily locatable within the application, such as a Data licences menu. [OSMF interactive-map guidance](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines#Interactive_maps)

## Ambiguity and recommendation

**Inference:** clearly labelled in-app Help could satisfy that fallback; a buried or external manual is not expressly approved. The guidance does not define inability to link or easy discoverability. Prefer making the visible credit link directly to [OSM copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), retaining Help/manual links as supplements. [OSMF interactive-map guidance](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines#Interactive_maps)

## Authority and limits

The guidelines describe a safe harbour, not the exclusive legal minimum. The checked page identifies revision 14786, last edited 2026-09-10. [OSMF revision](https://osmfoundation.org/w/index.php?title=Licence/Attribution_Guidelines&oldid=14786)

ODbL §4.3 requires an associated notice reasonably calculated to communicate the data source and licence. Its example uses hyperlinks, with plain URIs when hyperlinks are impossible; it does not expressly decide the Help/manual arrangement. This supports withholding a definitive legal pass or failure for that layout. [ODbL 1.0, §4.3](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)
