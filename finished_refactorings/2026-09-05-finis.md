## Contract

- Submitting the exact word `finis` in manual search, ignoring case and outer
  whitespace, opens one Finis companion window. Other searches remain normal.
- The supplied 2D cartoon atlas supplies all 16 clockwise gaze directions.
  Pointer movement anywhere inside the companion window selects the nearest
  direction relative to his face; the center and pointer exit restore neutral.
