## Final contract

- Use the supplied atlas unchanged: neutral cell (6, 0), plus rows 9 and 10 for
  16 directions clockwise from up. Decode these 17 cells once.
- Filter magenta only within five source pixels of transparency, borrowing nearby
  opaque non-magenta colors. Preserve all alpha, non-magenta and interior pixels.

  unknown pointer return to neutral. No timers or pet background work.
