## Limits

The fallback parser deliberately stops when total parsing work reaches the
input-sized budget. A heavily overlapping malformed prefix can therefore hide
later valid previews; normal disjoint JPEG previews fit within that budget.
Work is linear in encoded input size with constant additional parser storage;
the existing maximum of sixteen retained candidates remains unchanged.
