## Findings and evidence

- Initial PR is draft. Its description is the spec; it has no linked issue.
- Spec finding: trimming after `dedupe(files)` leaves preparation allocation
  and traversal proportional to the entire list, even on a cancelled pass.
  Cap the retained work and seen set while preparing, stop at the unique-path
  limit, and check cancellation during preparation.
- Standards finding: the manuals describe preparing all favorite previews;
  document the first-256 policy in English and German.
- Verification gap: the new bound test starts with an empty cache; also check
  that a preview outside the admitted prefix is removed from an existing cache.
- Honest limits: the input list is caller-owned; finding 256 unique paths may
  traverse duplicates, with cancellation checked on every entry. Source decode
  serialization is per pass; cancelled external reads must return to retire.
  Existing offline-source retention and cancellation's no-sweep policy remain.
